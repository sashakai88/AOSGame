Rabbit Overview

Rabbit is a Beast Form with little combat power. Rabbits are stealthy, and their abilities often make them undetected.

The Rabbit skill was first obtained by completing Therese's quest during the special weekend event on 10th June 2017. The reward for that quest was a Bunny Juice and an Un-Bunny Juice. After steam release these item's were raffled multiple times by guides in game.

The Bun-Fu Training special weekend event was first added in early November 2019. It provides a quest line yielding rabbit experience and several Bunny and Un-Bunny juices. It occurs once a year.

In later years, The Errana Bunny's Egg Hunt event occurs yearly around Easter, allowing players to acquire a Bunny and Unbunny juice and find eggs for an extra reward.

In-Game Description

The art of living and fighting as a rabbit or hare. Note: rabbits are not known for their ferocity, so sentient rabbits should carefully consider their choice of secondary skill.

Training Rabbit

  • Cinnamon the rabbit in Sun Vale unlocks levels 51-60 and levels 61-70.
  • Noita the Green in the Fae Realm unlocks 71-80.
  • Levels 81-90 are unlocked by Big Footed Jim deep beneath Povus.

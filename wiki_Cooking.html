Cooking Overview

Cooking is a trade skill that allows studied individuals to produce various regenerative foods, drinks, and other consumables. Cooked food can be consumed, or gifted to various NPCs (eg. Marna and Fainor).

Tip : because you earn a special bonus the first time you use a recipe, don't "spam" a recipe to level up! Try to buy a variety of recipes, and make one of each. Similarly consider eating one of each foods that you cooked, for the first time bonus Gourmand XP.

Skill in preparing food for consumption.

Training Cooking

  • Fainor in Serbule Keep sells a large number of recipes. Most of the recipes require to be in proximity of a stove or fire pit, but not all of them (typically preparing fish and meat as a base for other recipes, can be done anywhere).
  • Durstin Tallow teaches some level 3-24 recipes.
  • Mandibles teaches various Casino- and mantis-themed recipes from level 30-63.
  • Glowing Sphere teaches the recipes for Yeti Stick and Furpot.
  • Hiral in Sun Vale teaches some recipes for various beverages.
  • Squidlips in Sun Vale teaches some fish-related recipes level 25-45 and two recipes 70 & 72.
  • Laura Neth teaches some level 30-45 recipes, including a level 45 snack.
  • Selaxi unlocks Levels 51-60 and teaches recipes between those levels.
  • Nishika teaches various cat-based recipes
  • Floxie in Fae Realm unlocks both Levels 61-70 and 71-80 ,and teaches recipes between those levels.
  • Crelpin in the Fae Realm teaches various recipes (mostly fruit-based) in the range of levels 61-83.
  • Levels 81-90 are unlocked by Dunahain Broadarm deep beneath Povus.

Connected Skills

Sub-Skills:
  • Sushi Preparation - Requires Level 25 Cooking.
Related Skills:
  • Gardening - Most Cooking recipes require items grown with Gardening.
  • Butchering - Meat can be gathered through Butchering.
  • Foraging - Fruits can be gathered through Foraging
  • Cheesemaking - Cheese is sometimes needed as an ingredient.
  • Fishing - There are many recipes based around fish!

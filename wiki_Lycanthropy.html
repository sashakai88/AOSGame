Lycanthropy Overview

A Lycanthrope's Beast form makes for a very powerful animal form. You'll be able to change to and from wolf form at will, with the following limitations:

  • Characters with Lycanthropy may become a wolf at any time, instantly. However, to become humanoid (or your alternate beast form) again, wolves must kill a certain number of enemies before their beast spirit will now allow them to un-transform. The higher your Lycanthropy level, the fewer enemies you need to kill to un-transform, until at Lycanthropy level 53, you can switch back and forth without any killing.
  • During the full moon, Lycans are forcefully turned into wolf form and cannot un-transform for the three days of the full moon. This happens once every lunar month, matching the real life moon cycles. If your Lycanthropy-infected character was not in wolf form and offline when the full moon began, during the full moon you will be transformed into the default brown-fur wolf form immediately upon logging in. If you have unlocked and want to be in the Winter Wolf or Dark Wolf form during the full moon, you need to be in that form before the full moon phase begins.
  • Remember that turning into a Lycanthrope is a PERMANENT CHARACTER ALTERATION THAT CANNOT BE UNDONE and will NEVER be made reversible (the developers have repeatedly confirmed this). For this reason, it is highly advised you carefully consider the pros and cons before making the decision. For three days every month, Lycans are forced to play as a wolf, and several NPCs and vendors will not speak to wolves. Some can be persuaded to speak with wolves after a particular bribe or a short quest, but a few will not offer any options at all. This situation can by mitigated by using an alternate character and the transfer chest to sell items or by finding similar vendors in other zones who will speak to you or have a wolf bribe quest.

Advantages

  • Lycanthropy is one of the only few animal forms that can shift in and out of their humanoid or alternate beast form at will (except during full moons). And the transformation is instantaneous.
  • The Beast Metabolism ability, in which you can consume the corpses of your defeated enemies rather than burying them, provides a unique temporary health regeneration buff and, when leveled, significant boosts in overall Metabolism.
  • The Howling ability, which grants health regeneration boosts and damage boosts—a buff which can last for up to five hours if the howling is done with additional howling wolves present.
  • During the full moon you have unique quest objectives that serve as an interesting alternative to typical skill progression.
  • Like all beast forms, Lycans (when in wolf form) can learn and use the Warden combat skill.
  • Wolves can wear and benefit from crafted wolf-specific metal armor (see Armorsmithing).

Disadvantages

  • Lycanthropy is an Animal Form. Like all animal forms, you have no hands and many NPCs will refuse to talk to you. A few animals (such as Red in Kur) may not talk to you either or lose favor if you try to speak with them because you are a scary animal. The defensive pylons at the Fox Camp in Kur will also attack you.
  • For the three days of the full moon you will be unable to return to your human form no matter what. Words of power will work for their duration.
  • You cannot chop wood or milk cows while in wolf form. (Again, having a humanoid alternate character to deal with these matters when you are in wolf form can be handy, pun intended.)
  • Secondary combat skills while in wolf form are limited to Animal Handling, Mentalism, Necromancy, Priest, Psychology, Unarmed, or Warden because all the other combat skills require a specific enabling held weapon. Weapons for other skills are not recognized as being equipped while in wolf form, although any bonuses or mods on the primary or secondary weapon items do still apply.

Lycanthropy Mechanics

  • Once a player becomes a werewolf, they'll be especially vulnerable to silver and wolfsbane, both in humanoid and beast forms. Eating food with wolfsbane or holding a silver weapon will harm werewolves.
  • During the three days before full moon, werewolves receive the Lunacy buff, which increases their max health by +30. (Both in your humanoid and beast forms)
  • Wolves have the ability to feed on enemy corpses for a regeneration buff that becomes more powerful based on Beast Metabolism
  • The ability to howl increases a wolf's (out and in) combat health regeneration and damage. It will last up to an hour for a single wolf or for example 5 hours for a group of 5 wolves. Werewolves that howl together boost both effects and max duration by - +1h +2hp regen and +1 damage for each wolf howling in proximity. Repeatedly howling will also eventually get wolves experience on the Howling sub-skill.

The Full Moon

  • During a real life full Moon players will be stuck as a werewolf. If players are in white wolf form before the full moon you still stay in this form which is preferable due to the form's cold weather mitigation.
  • If wolves try to change forms during the full moon they will be stuck as the default brown wolf. Players can not change from normal wolf form to white wolf form during the event.
  • The full moon is an event. Go to Kur Mountains zone and enter the Wolf Cave at the Far north and center of the map. Directly Inside the cave is an Altar of Norala. Completing the altar quests will earn tokens. These tokens disappear if not used during the current full moon. They unlock permanent buffs like 50-60 lycanthrope, 60-70 lycanthrope, and dark wolf form or unlock month long buffs like +%damage, +health, +armor and the coveted +12 run speed bonus buff.
  • It is advisable to unlock the dark wolf form last. The number of tokens is limited by the number of quests. Unlocking the level caps and werewolf abilities should be a priority since tokens are "time gated" by the full moon.

Altar of Norala

During the full moon, werewolves may now interact with the Altar of Norala placed at the entrance of the Wolf Cave in Kur Mountains. The Altar performs several tasks for the goddess of the hunt, which will earn them tokens they can then spend on special abilities, unlocks and boons.

  • The tokens must be used during the full moon or they will disappear.
  • The Boons and Runspeed buffs that are unlocked with tokens will last until the next Real Life full moon.

Trivia

  • Lycanthropy has no cure! There will never, ever be a cure. Be 100% certain you want your character to be a Lycan before committing to it.

Notes

Sanja can unlock Lycanthropy levels 51-60 and 61-70 for councils. Alternatively (and rather less expensively), these level unlocks are also available at the Altar of Norala during the full moon for task reward tokens. Similarly, See Red 6 and See Red 7 are also available from Sanja for councils, or from the Altar of Norala during the full moon for tokens. You may want to schedule your training options accordingly.

If your character is a Druid and can transform into a deer, assuming it is not the full moon, you can use that to speak freely with NPCs who will speak with animals but are afraid of scary wolves. Transforming directly from wolf to deer (and back again) still counts as being in continuous beast form in terms of the 3-hour in-game beast form status required to speak to most Animal Town residents. (Druid bird forms also work.)

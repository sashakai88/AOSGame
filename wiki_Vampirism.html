Vampirism Overview

Vampirism is intended for experienced players and it requires many high level group quests to fully unlock. Once you start you also have to deal with the sun burning you. In exchange you get access to a skill with high potential for Trauma, Psychic and Darkness damage with exceptional crowd control abilities. At the moment it is possible to reverse the vampirism but this may be removed in the future.

In-Game Description

Vampin'

Training Vampirism

  • Truxartis inflicts this skill upon the willing after completing a couple of quests. He also provides the majority of the training up to Level 70. He can be found underneath a tower in the northeastern mountains in Vidaria
  • Bendith the Banished can unlock 71-80.
  • Levels 81-90 are unlocked by Reinath deep beneath Povus.

Curing Vampirism

  • Vampirism can be removed from a character by interacting with a lever inside one of the vampire safehouses, accessible as part of the Accessing the Safehouse quest.

Related Skills:

Here are some skills that pair well with Vampirism:

  • Knife Fighting. This pairing has access to very high trauma damage and trauma vulnerability application. Vampirisms crowd control is great for knife fighting while soloing as well.
  • Lycanthropy is another potent option for a trauma damage build assuming you don't mind the downsides. Vampirism also has a couple of mod that buff biting damage which grants additional potential.
  • Giant Bat is another potential option. It has access to many trauma mods and has synergy with a mod the makes Embrace of despair Sonic damage. It also has skills that work with the Vampirism bite mods
  • Psychology. A fantastic option for a psychic damage pairing, Vampirism mods provide a Psychic vulnerability and its psychic attacks can even work with Phrenology crits with the right mods.
  • Spirit Fox. A great option for a darkness damage build. Vampirism also has darkness vulnerability access and spirit fox will also enjoy the bite mods
  • Necromancy. Darkness/psychic damage over time build.
  • Mentalism. Psychic damage build. Both direct and over time.

How to Play (AOS 4th Edition Rules):

  • Battle Structure:
    • Battle Rounds → 2 Turns per Round → 6 Phases per Turn
    • Phases: Hero, Movement, Shooting, Charge, Combat, Turn End
    • Command Points reset to 4 each battle round
  • Objectives & Victory:
    • Golden markers are objectives - contest within 3"
    • Control value determines who controls each objective
    • Each controlled objective = 1 VP per Turn End Phase
  • Weapons & Abilities:
    • Crit (2 Hits): Critical hit (unmodified 6) = 2 hits
    • Crit (Auto-wound): Critical hit auto-wounds
    • Anti-X (+1 Rend): +1 Rend vs keyword (e.g., INFANTRY)
    • Charge (+1 Damage): +1 Damage if unit charged
    • Ward X+: Save after normal save (e.g., 6+ ward)
  • Movement Options:
    • Move: Normal move up to MOVE characteristic
    • Run: Move + D6, cannot shoot this turn
    • Retreat: Move away from combat, take D3 mortal damage, cannot shoot/charge
    • Charge: Roll 2D6, move that distance toward enemy
  • Combat Rules:
    • Units in combat (within 3") cannot move or shoot
    • Can only retreat to escape combat
    • Attack sequence: Hit → Wound → Save → Ward → Damage
    • Unmodified 1s always fail hit/wound/save rolls
  • Auto-Skip: Phases skip in 1s when no valid actions available!

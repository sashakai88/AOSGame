How to Play (AOS 4th Edition Rules):

  • Instant Auto-Skip: Phases skip in 1 second when no valid actions!
    • Hero Phase: Skips instantly (no abilities yet)
    • Shooting Phase: Skips if no ranged units or enemies out of range
    • Charge Phase: Skips if no enemies in charge range
    • Combat Phase: Skips if no units in melee range (3")
    • Timer shows "Auto" when auto-skipping - no waiting!
  • Unit Ranges: Check unit cards for weapon ranges
    • Melee units have "Range: Melee" (must be within 3")
    • Ranged units show shooting distance (e.g., "Range: 18"")
    • Some units have both ranged and melee capabilities
  • Timer: 30 seconds per active phase. Auto-skip phases bypass timer!
  • Phase-Based Actions: Just click your units! The game knows what to do:
    • Movement Phase: Click unit → Click destination
    • Shooting Phase: Click ranged unit → Click enemy in range
    • Charge Phase: Click unit → Click near enemy to charge
    • Combat Phase: Click unit → Click enemy within 3" to fight
  • Combat Range: 3" around each model (shown as golden circle)
  • Attack Sequence: Hit → Wound → Save → Damage
  • Critical Hits: Unmodified 6s deal +1 bonus damage!

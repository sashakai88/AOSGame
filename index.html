How to Play:

  • Select Unit: Click on your unit cards or battlefield units
  • Movement: Click "Move Unit" then click destination
  • Charge: Move towards enemy to engage in melee
  • Attack: Select your unit, click "Attack", select enemy within 3"
  • Combat: Hit Roll → Wound Roll → Save Roll → Damage
  • Critical Hits: Unmodified 6s trigger special effects!

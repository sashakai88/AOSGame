Carpentry Overview

Carpentry is the skill of wood working, it is used to craft chairs, some weapons, and food accessories.

If you plan on gathering wood yourself, you'll need to buy a Handsaw from a vendor such as Therese in Serbule town. Alternatively, players of a high level may encounter a Cutting Blade Necklace, an amulet slot item that also works as a handsaw.

Note: Under no circumstances can any regular animal beast-form gather wood from regular tree-falls, not even with the Cutting Blade Necklace. Birds can, but only if they are humanoid under the Druid illusion. For animals, the only options are to use alternate humanoid characters to gather wood, buy it from players, break it down from Treant Wood loot, or barter with Gretchen Salas. Alternatively, you might find it in NPC Used Item tabs or you can try posting player work orders.

In-Game Description

Preparing and treating lumber, and making things out of wood.

Training Carpentry

  • Jara in Serbule will teach Carpentry to those who help her with a bear problem. She teaches most basic recipes for Carpentry.
  • Lawara on Anagoge gives Carpentry XP when the hangout Help carve marionettes is completed.
  • Paul Vaughn in Serbule Hills teaches basic calligraphy bench recipes and a few other recipes.
  • The Wombat in Sun Vale can teach several recipes. Wooden Melee Staffs, Combat Staffs, and Wooden shoes for Cows and Deer can be learned from him.
  • Lamashu in the Kur Mountains teaches recipes for Storage Crates. She also teaches the combat abilities in the Stake the Heart set.
  • Ufkar in Rahu teaches the recipes for Battle Chemistry staves.
  • Amutasa, a toolmaker in Rahu, can raise the skill cap for Carpentry from 51-60 and 61-70.
  • Solgribue unlocks 71-80.
  • Levels 81-90 are unlocked by Dunahain Broadarm deep beneath Povus.

Connected Skills

Sub-Skills:
  • None
Secondary Skills:
  • Toolcrafting - Toolcrafting requires Level 20 Carpentry.
Related Skills:
  • Blacksmithing - Some Carpentry recipes require materials that can be crafted by Blacksmiths.
  • Fletching - A successful Fletcher will find themselves needing many materials crafted by Carpentry.
  • Foraging - A Carpenter needs a source of wood, and Foraging allows wood to be gathered.

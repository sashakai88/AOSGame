Treasure Effects

From Project: Gorgon Wiki
Jump to: navigation, search

Treasure Effects are skill, stat, and ability altering effects found on looted or crafted pieces of equipment or crafted.

  • Every Combat Skill has its own set of Treasure Effects.
  • Some non-combat skills such as Armor Patching, Gourmand, and Fishing also have Treasure Effects that can appear on equipment.
  • Treasure Effects on an item can be altered with Transmutation, Augmentation, and Shamanic Infusion.

Hunting Quests

From Project: Gorgon Wiki
Jump to: navigation, search
An example of a creature with a Crown Hunting symbol.

Hunting Quests are a mechanic first introduced in March of 2016. Sometimes, an Adventurer will encounter a creature with a silver crown glowing over its head. Defeating this creature will grant the Adventurer a quest. Upon completion, the player receives experience points in their two active combat skills, plus xp in the related anatomy type. In addition, if the player uses First Aid and/or Armor Patching while completing the final (usually tenth) kill of a hunting quest, the combat xp from completing the quest will be awarded for those skills as well.

Serbule Hills Hunting Quests

  • Hunting: Black Widow Spiders This Hunting Quest calls for the elimination of Black Widow Spiders, which are are common across Serbule Hills.
    + Black Widow Spiders are found throughout the entire region, except for directly around the Ranalon-controlled lakes.
    • Kill Spiders x10
  • Hunting: Deer

    This Hunting Quest calls for the elimination of Deer in Serbule Hills.
    + Buck Deer, Doe Deer, and the rarer Rabid Deer can be found in the region, usually on hillsides.

    • Kill Deer x10
  • Hunting: Fire Sheep

    This Hunting Quest calls for the elimination of Fire Sheep, which are are a rare spawn in Serbule Hills.
    + Fire Sheep are found predominately on hillsides and at the top of hills. The western edge of the region is a common place to find them, as are the hills in the southwest.

    • Kill Fire Sheep x5
  • Hunting: Goblins

    This Hunting Quest calls for the elimination of Goblins, which are are part of an invasion force encountered at encampments throughout the eastern half of Serbule Hills.
    + Commonly encountered Goblins include: Camp Overseer, Competent Goblin, Goblin Archer, Goblin Archery Student, Goblin Guard, Goblin Lumberjack, Goblin Patrol, Goblin Scribe, Goblin Soldier, Goblin Spear Trainee, Goblin Spearman, Logging Camp Guard.

    • Kill Goblins in Serbule Hills x10
  • Hunting: Guard Scrays

    This Hunting Quest calls for the elimination of Guard Scrays, which are Ranalon-bred pets found throughout their encampments in Serbule Hills.
    + Guard Scrays are found on the shores of the two Ranalon-controlled lakes, although occasionally a few may fly further north.

    • Kill Guard Scrays x8
  • Hunting: Pigs

    This Hunting Quest calls for the elimination of Pigs, which are are common near farmland inSerbule Hills.
    + Pigs are found primarily in the northern hills and western hills. Sometimes, pigs will be found near patches of farmland.

    • Kill Pigs x10
  • Hunting: Ranalon Gardeners

    This Hunting Quest calls for the elimination of Ranalon Gardeners, a frequently encountered variety of Ranalon in Serbule Hills.
    + Ranalon Gardeners are found both underwater in the two lakes, and on the beaches around them.

    • Kill Ranalon Gardeners x10
  • Hunting: Ranalon Stone Shapers

    This Hunting Quest calls for the elimination of Ranalon Stone-Shapers, which are part o the hostile Ranalon invasion of Serbule Hills.
    + Ranalon Stone-Shapers are found among the hills surrounding the two lakes.

    • Kill Ranalon Stoneshapers x6
  • Hunting: Rats

    This Hunting Quest calls for the elimination of Rats, which are a common species encountered throughout Serbule Hills and the rest of Alharth.
    + Giant Plains Rats are found throughout the entire region, especially in the northwest.

    • Kill Rats x10
  • Hunting: Sheep

    This Hunting Quest calls for the elimination of Valley Sheep, unexpectedly dangerous denizens of Serbule Hills.
    + Valley Sheep are found throughout the entire region, and can easily be found everywhere without extensive tree cover.

    • Kill Sheep x10
  • Hunting: Tigers

    This Hunting Quest calls for the elimination of Tigers, which are are common across Serbule Hills.
    + Tigers and Sickly Tigers are found in both the northern hills of the region and the eastern forestland.

    • Kill Tigers x10
  • Hunting: Wild Chickens

    This Hunting Quest calls for the elimination of Wild Chickens, found around farmland in Serbule Hills.
    + Wild Chicken are found predominantly near the farmlands and buildings in the northwest.

    • Kill Wild Chickens x6
  • Hunting: Wild Pigs
    • Kill Wild Pigs x10
  • Hunting: Worgs

    This Hunting Quest calls for the elimination of Worgs, which are are usually uncommon, but often seen in the southern hills of Serbule Hills.
    + Worgs are found primarily in the hills south of the two lakes, but can be occasionally seen in the northeast.

    • Kill Worgs x10

Eltibule Hunting Quests

  • Hunting: Cunning Wolves This Hunting Quest calls for the elimination of Cunning Wolves, which are are common foes in Eltibule.

Archery Overview

Archery is a combat skill often learned by many across Alharth. A basic skill used by hunters, farmers, and warriors alike, Archery focuses on providing ranged attacks that can cover a wide area.

In-Game Description

Expertise with using bows to hunt and kill.

Training Archery

  • An old rumor suggests that Archery was one of many skills taught on the Island of Anagoge, once home to a Council Academy. Travelers whisper of a magical bow empowered with the ability to instantly teach the skill of Archery to anyone who successfully finds it.
  • Elahil is Serbule's resident Bowyer & Fletcher. For a price, he can train travelers in the basics of Archery.
  • Alravesa the All-Hunter is willing to teach experienced Archers more advanced techniques. Found in the remote Town of Amulna, Alravesa will help her friends unlock Levels 51-60 and 61-70 of Archery. She is also willing to teach advanced abilities to those willing and able to learn them.
  • Floxie in the Fae Realm unlocks Archery Skill Levels 71-80 and teaches level 70+ abilities.
  • Levels 81-90 are unlocked by Emersil the Ever-Gloating deep beneath Povus.

Connected Skills

Sub-Skills:
  • None
Secondary Skills:
  • Fletching - The craft of arrow-making. This crafting skill is generally picked up by any Archer wanting to produce their own arrows.
Related Skills:
  • Alchemy - The corrosive Acidic Cleaner series of alchemical liquids is often used in Fletching, so Archers interested in crafting arrows should study this skill.
  • Carpentry - A skill that goes hand-in-hand with Fletching, and produces some materials required by Fletching.

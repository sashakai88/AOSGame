Survival Instincts Overview

Survival Instincts is a sidebar skill for animal-characters that provides utility skills and a replacement for armor patching.

In-Game Description

The ability of self-aware animals to enhance their natural instincts into finely-honed combat techniques. Only animals can use this skill.

Training Survival Instincts

  • Mittens from the Fox Outpost in Kur Mountains teaches Survival Instincts at Comfortable.
  • Sanja, also located in the Kur Mountains unlocks levels 51-60, 61-70, and teaches Lend Grace and abilities for levels 51-70.
  • Backfat in the Fae Realm unlocks level 71-80 and teaches techniques in that level range.
  • Levels 81-90 are unlocked by Serendipity Cheddar deep beneath Povus.

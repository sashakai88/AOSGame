Psychology Overview

Psychology is a strange skill that revolves around affecting allies/enemies by getting into their head. It's one of the most versatile support skills, with a mix of crowd control, healing, and rage reduction.

In-Game Description

Understanding the mind of sentient creatures. Also, using that knowledge in combat and other stressful situations.

Training Psychology

  • Riger will not directly teach you Psychology, but after you complete his quest, he will allow you to read a Psychology book.
  • For players that do not want to go back to the island to learn Psychology, Yetta sells a book titled Fighting with Psychology. Alternatively completing the "alternative approach to continued slaughter..." for Oritania's quest Kill Spiders will award Psychology experience and unlock it.
  • Helena Veilmoor and Suspicious Cow teach You Were Adopted 1-3 and But I Love You 1-2
  • Nissim in Amulna will unlock Psychology 51-60, 61-70, and teaches level 51+ abilities.
  • Raina unlocks 71-80.
  • Levels 81-90 are unlocked by Emersil the Ever-Gloating deep beneath Povus.

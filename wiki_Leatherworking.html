Leatherworking Overview

Leatherworking is a craft that uses leather bits produced by Tanning to create usable leather clothing. This skill is also quite beneficial for creating clothing to survive in cold, harsh climates.

In-Game Description

Turning leathers into armor and goods.

Training Leatherworking

  • Kleave in Eltibule is willing to train her [friends] the basics of leatherworking.
  • Sirine in Rahu unlocks levels 51-60 and 61-70.
  • Ukorga in Kur Mountains trains travelers in crafting cold weather gear.
  • Felmer will unlock levels 71-80 and teaches 80+ recipes.
  • Levels 81-90 are unlocked by Dunahain Broadarm deep beneath Povus.

Connected Skills

Sub-Skills:

Secondary Skills:
  • Tanning - Tanning is used to tan animal skins for use in Leatherworking.
  • Skinning - Skinning is used to collect animal skins to be used with Tanning.

Note About Recipes

Kleave's recipes are now sold in "bundles", and you won't be able to purchase the recipes until you have the level needed to complete all of them. For example, you cannot purchase Decent Leatherworking recipes until you have 33 Leatherworking (required for Decent Leather Shirt), even though only 30 Leatherworking is required to perform some of the Decent recipes.

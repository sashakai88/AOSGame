Bard Overview

Bard is a skill intended for Adventurers looking to provide support to their fellow travelers. The Bard skillset can only be used by humanoid races at this time, and travelers confined to Beast Forms will find it difficult, even impossible to use.

In-Game Description

Bardin'

Training Bard

  • Rappanel is a teacher for beginner Bards, and can be found along the old trade-route trail in Serbule Hills, inside the Tapestry Inn.
  • Percussa can raise the level cap of Bard from 51-60, and from 61-70. She can be found in Rahu. During the day she performs in front of the restaurant and in the evening she returns home to the hostile part of the city.
  • Raina unlocks 71-80.
  • Levels 81-90 are unlocked by Emersil the Ever-Gloating deep beneath Povus.

Ability overview

  • Thunderous Note: Simple attack for Lute bards, can be modded for higher attack range. Can be modded to be nature damage and debuff enemies to take more damage from nature.
  • Entrancing Lullaby: Mezmerize an enemy, crowd control.
  • Rally: Major Healing for a single target. Can be modded to restore power and armor.
  • Song of Resurgence: Constant healing for all allies near you. Can be modded to restore power too. Can be modded to increase your major healing power.
  • Song of Discord: Powerful constant direct trauma damage to all enemies, medium range. Can be modded to reduce rage and/or stun. Can be modded to increase damage every time you are attacked.
  • Anthem of Avoidance: Support, give all allies evasion to projectiles. Can be modded for melee and burst evasion.
  • Moment of Resolve: Support, restore armor and reduce damage taken. Can be modded to give stun, slow, root immunity.

Bard Mechanics

Bards can play with a lute and/or horn. Horn abilities focus on dealing damage, lute abilities are songs (see below).

Songs

Bards using a lute can play a song for constant effects like healing or damage. When moving, the song is interrupted so it is a good idea to pull groups of enemies or have a high range attack to pull. Switching to a different song will interrupt the current song.

Weapons

Lute instruments are all MainHand, while everything else is OffHand. Even though only the Lutes are tagged as Wooden, Druids may use all instruments.

Hymn of Resurrection

Bards have access to the revival ability Hymn of Resurrection. To unlock and use this ability, a Bard must be wearing a Main Hand item with the proper treasure effect. The required treasure only appears on Epic or Legendary equipment at Levels 50 and 70. Although the ability can be learned from any Main Hand item with the treasure, a Lute with the treasure must be held to actually use the ability.

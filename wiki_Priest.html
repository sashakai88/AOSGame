Priest Overview

Priest is a combat skill.

In-Game Description

Officially the 'Priest of Arisetsu' skill, this represents your attunement with Arisetsu, the goddess of Warmth and Hope. When fighting alongside allies, you can bring hope to even the most impossible battles... but you cannot do it alone. You must be wearing a holy symbol -- and NOT wearing a Necromancy Gem -- to use Priest abilities. Holy symbols are most often found as jewelry, but can also be found on armor and weapons.

Training Priest

  • Gloria Stonecurl in Kur Mountains teaches Priest. You must have either 25 Levels of Compassion or Psychology to learn the skill, In addition to [Comfortable] status with Gloria. There is also a fee of 5,000 councils.
  • Aralorn in Gazluk unlocks levels 51-60, 61-70.
  • Raina unlocks 71-80.
  • Levels 81-90 are unlocked by Reinath deep beneath Povus.

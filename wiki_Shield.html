Shield Overview

Shield is a defensive skill that focuses on damage mitigation and holding aggro.

In-Game Description

Expertise in the use of a shield, both as a weapon and as a defensive tool.

Training Shield

  • Tyler Green teaches Shield at [Comfortable].
  • Nelson Ballard teaches Rapid Recovery 1-6 and Fire Shield 1-4.
  • Gorvessa in Hogan's Keep teaches many Shield abilities, see the ability table below.
  • Sir Johnson will unlock Shield levels 51-60, 61-70, and teaches level 51+ abilities.
  • Pegast unlocks 71-80, and teaches the corresponding abilities.
  • Levels 81-90 are unlocked by Emersil the Ever-Gloating deep beneath Povus. The abilities for these levels are all dropped from Nightmare Caves, or bartered from Errruka.

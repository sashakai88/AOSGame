Crossbow Overview

Crossbow is a special combat sidebar skill that can be used together with 2 other 'main' skills.It requires you to be holding a crossbow in your Offhand. It offers decent damage with long cooldowns.

In-Game Description

Knowledge of crossbows including how to fire and reload them, as well as how to maintain them in peak condition.

Training Crossbow

  • Bellema Deftwhisper will teach you at Comfortable favor level. She also unlocks levels 51-60 and teaches the various Crossbow abilities.
  • Floxie unlocks levels 61-70 and levels 71-80.
  • Levels 81-90 are unlocked by Serendipity Cheddar deep beneath Povus.

Connected Skills

Related Skills

  • Fletching - You need this to craft the arrows that Crossbow abilities consume.
Synergy Levels

Bonus Synergy Levels for Crossbow can be obtained from the following skills and levels:

Hammer Overview

Hammer is a primary Combat Skill. Hammer focuses mainly on dealing Crushing damage, but can also deal Electricity damage and Trauma damage. There are not many abilities, but that makes it a simple and straightforward skill for learning and mastering. Hammer consumes quite a lot of Power but deals high damage. Hammer focuses on burst damage, this means it's excellent for taking down enemies quickly.

Hammer allows the player to wield wooden clubs and metal hammers. There is no big difference between these two types of weapons. All abilities can be used with a club or a hammer. The only exception is a player using the Hammer and Druid combat skills; they can only use wooden clubs.

In-Game Description

Mastery of the use of Hammers and clubs in combat. Power consumption is higher than average, but so is damage. Hammers excel at rapidly murdering enemies, no more and no less.

Training Hammer

  • Agrashab teaches Hammer at [Friends] and teaches some low level abilities.
  • Falkrin Overstrike will unlock levels 51-60, 61-70, and teaches level 51+ abilities
  • Solgribue will unlock levels 71-80, and teaches level 71+ abilities
  • Levels 81-90 are unlocked by Emersil the Ever-Gloating deep beneath Povus.

Special abilities

Most abilities are just normal damaging abilities, but some of them have special mechanics:

No power cost but Armor cost Reckless Slam is an attack that doesn't drain Power, but drains the player's Armor instead if they still have Armor remaining.
Damage over time Rib Shatter deals a high amount of damage and causes the enemy to take Trauma damage over 20 seconds.
Stun Leaping Smash damages and knocks the enemy down. Enemy takes 25% additional damage from all attacks while knocked down.
Slow Seismic Impact damages the enemy and slows them by 90%.
Recover Armor and Power Look At My Hammer restores a large amount of Armor and a small amount of Power. Costs no Power.

 Belt of the Woodland Warrior increases damage dealt with Hammer and Druid combat skills by 6%. Can be bought from Agrashab.

 Belt of the Barbarian increases damage dealt with Hammer and Shield combat skills by 6%. Can be bought from Agrashab.

 Belt of the Mindfist increases damage dealt with Hammer and Mentalism combat skills by 6%. Can be bought from Agrashab.

 Belt of the Hammerer can be crafted. Requires 40 Toolcrafting.


Additionally, players can craft buckles with the Buckle Artistry skill. This skill involves crafting unique Belts that give bonuses to Hammer users only. These buckles are attuned to the crafter, that means that they can only be equipped by the player who made them. Unlike the belts listed above, buckles have durability and and will degrade over time while in combat, eventually disappearing.

Fairy Magic Overview

Fairy Magic is a Mentalism sub-skill used by Fae adventurers.

In-Game Description

A sub-skill of Mentalism that focuses on psychic links to the fae realms.

Training Fairy Magic

  • Preta in Sun Vale offers the Level 51-60 and Level 61-70 unlocks.
  • Raina in the Fae Realm offers the Level 71-80 unlock.
  • Levels 81-90 are unlocked by Reinath deep beneath Povus.

.

This attuned gemstone can be used in any recipe which calls for the use of Crystals to create equipment with Fairy Magic Treasure Effects.

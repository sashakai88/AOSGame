Cheesemaking Overview

Cheesemaking is a time intensive skill revolving around the production of cheeses.

Cheese is marked as a Prepared Food and as a Dairy Dish for purposes of gift giving (ie. earning favor from NPCs).

In-Game Description

Making cheese, butter, and other dairy products. But mostly: cheese.

Training Cheesemaking

  • Braigon is a Cheesemaker in Eltibule, and is willing to share his craft. Braigon can also raise the level cap from 51-60. The first (Level 0) recipe (Butter) can be learned from Braigon.
  • Bendith the Banished is the trainer in Fae Realm. He can unlock levels 61-70 [Comfortable], 71-80 [Like Family], and trains various high level recipes.
  • Hogan, Laura Neth, Mandibles, and Hiral can train a few random recipes as well.
  • A few can only be learned from scrolls

Connected Skills

Sub-Skills:
  • None
Secondary Skills:
  • Cooking
Related Skills:
  • Mycology - Cheesemakers will want to learn Mycology, as Rennet is produced through this skill.
  • Cow - Cows can make Milk, a necessary ingredient for Cheesemaking.
  • Carpentry - Carpenters can craft the Firkins and Kilderkins used in Cheesemaking.
  • Textile Creation - Cheesemaking requires textiles such as Butter Muslins and Cheesecloths.
Synergy Levels:

Bonus Synergy Levels for Cheesemaking can be obtained from the following skills and levels:

Cow
Level 19, Level 41

Cheesemaking Mechanics

Caves

A few Cheesemaking recipes require
 
Kilderkin
or
 
Firkin
. These cheeses must be aged in caves before they can be consumed.
For example, a
 
Firkin of Camembert Cheese
must be placed in a cool, damp cave for 2 hours.

Caves that are "cool and damp" are those that have water in them.
Some locations where cheese can be aged: Crystal Cavern, Myconian Cave, Brain Bug Cave, New Prestonbule Cave, Wolf Cave, Serbule Hills Spider Cave.
Some locations where cheese can not be aged: Rahu Sewer, Yeti Cave

Aging

Upon completing aging a cask, you will receive some experience (in addition to that received when crafting the cask, if you had crafted it yourself). This "second round" of experience is unaffected by crafting experience buffs. For casks that produce a variable amount of cheese, the experience received depends on the amount of cheese produced.

Please note, this table is a work-in-progress. Yield of aging may depend on the Cheesemaking Level of the character aging the cask. Note that it is possible to age a cask without possessing the Cheesemaking Level required to produce it. Currently the table reflects the yield when the character aging the cask is exactly the minimum level to produce it (if there are no errors).

Stomachs

A Cheesemaker will often find themselves in need of a Rennet for their recipes.

 
Basic Rennet
,
 
Strong Rennet
, and
 
Pungent Rennet
are crafted through Mycology, and require a supply of
 
Stomach
s to make.

Whey and Sweet Whey

Some Cheesemaking recipes call for a
 
Bottle of Whey
or
 
Bottle of Sweet Whey
. These are acidic byproducts of the Cheesemaking process, and certain cheese recipes will produce them.

Whey and sweet whey can be stacked up to ten per slot.

Soybeans & Tofu

Level 35 cheesemakers can start an arduous journey towards producing Tofu by learning the recipe for
 
Bottle Of Soy Milk
.
 
Soybeans
, the primary ingredient of
 
Bottle Of Soy Milk
, can only be naturally harvested in the wilds of Povus. When properly processed, Tofu can be used in various Cooking recipes.
  •  
    Soybeans
    can be gathered from Wild Soybean bushes.
  • Wild Soybean bushes grow throughout the Fall Harvest season.
  • Outside of the harvest season, bushes grow only during the New Moon.
  • Wild Soybean bushes can also be harvested for a
     
    Soybean Sprout
    , which can later be planted and harvested from a garden.
Tofu Press
Converting
 
Bottle Of Soy Milk
into Tofu requires a Tofu Press.
  • During the Fall Harvest, two Tofu Press can be found near Ricatu in the City of Povus.
  • Three Tofu Press are hidden in the cellars of the Goblin Dungeon under Eltibule Keep.

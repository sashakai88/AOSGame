Cosmetic Pet

From Project: Gorgon Wiki
Jump to: navigation, search

Cosmetic Pets are small magical creatures an adventurer can learn to summon. Cosmetic Pets can only perform the Cosmetic Pet Attack which deals almost no damage, and they quickly expire in combat when targeted by enemies. Only one Cosmetic Pet can be summoned at a time.

As of the 3 December 2023 update, Cosmetic Pets when summoned are persistent (until dismissed) even through zone transitions and logout/login.

Cosmetic Pets are generally difficult to obtain. Many are distributed only during Seasonal Events in a particular year, while others are possible rewards for the completion of reoccurring events such as the Crafting Caravan. A few of the Cosmetic Pets such as the Kraken appear to be Admin-only items.

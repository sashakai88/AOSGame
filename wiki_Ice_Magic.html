Ice Magic Overview

Ice Magic uses devastating attacks, combined with the randomness of weather and the freezing abilities of ice to create a deadly skill with medium-high power cost. Like other research based magics, Ice Magic abilities are learned through the sacrifice of ingredients to research spells.

In-Game Description

A specialization of Fire Magic that focuses entirely upon lowering temperature instead of raising it. It has protective and controlling abilities as well as offensive abilities.

Training Ice Magic

  • Ice Magic is taught by Landri the Cold after completing a quest inside Kur Tower. To begin this quest, a Prodigy should look for a warning sign posted directly inside the dungeon. One must also have Level 25 Fire Magic or Level 25 Rabbit.
  • Ichin the Ice Master can unlock Levels 51-70 Ice Magic, and teaches the associated recipes.
  • Bendith the Banished will have Levels 71-80 frozen into your brain.
  • Levels 81-90 are unlocked by Reinath deep beneath Povus.

Ability overview

  • Ice Spear: High range attack spell, great damage.
  • Ice Lightning: Electricity damage, must-have to fight ice resistant mobs.
  • Tundra Spikes: Mid-range AoE that locks mobs in place so you can get out of melee range and attack with ranged spells. Can be modded with stun.
  • Blizzard: High-range AoE, slows enemies while you retreat. Can be modded to reduce rage.
  • Frostbite: Randomized stun. Can be modded for more enemy debuffs like raising max rage or chance to miss.
  • Freeze Solid: Mesmerize an enemy when you are overwhelmed, but watch out when using AoEs or it will break.
  • Ice Nova: Short-range AoE, can be modded to heal.
  • Cold Protection: Sidebar self-only buff to protect from ice damage and reduce heat loss in cold areas.
  • Cryogenic Freeze: 10-second self-stun, only useful if you need a short break to recover health. Can be modded to recover health, armor, power.

Ice Magic Mechanics

Fairies

Fairies learn most Ice Magic (not the Glowing Sphere's Ice Veins 3B) automatically from leveling up Ice Magic. See Ice Magic Level Up Rewards.

Research Ingredients

Ice Magic can be viewed as expensive due to the ingredients required for spell research recipes. Each recipe unlocks a selection of abilities in a random order.

If you complete Ice Magic Research Level 25, you will learn one of six abilities (Blizzard 2, Chill 2, Cryogenic Freeze, Ice Armor 3, Ice Lightning 3, Tundra Spikes 2). Complete Ice Magic Research Level 25 six times to learn all associated abilities.

 
Ice Core
- Looted from various ice-themed creatures creatures, particularly elementals (Cold Elemental), frozen slimes (Ice Slick), and undead ice mages (Skeleton Ice Guard).
 
Tundra Lichen
- Harvested from the northern hills and shorefront of Kur Mountains. Requires level 37 Foraging.
 
Orcish Spell Pouch
- Looted from Orcish Thaumaturge and similar spellcaster orc.
 
Frost Nib
- Obtained from bartering with The Sand Seer in Ilmari.
 
Glowy Yellow Crystal
- Collected from nodes in set points around Gazluk. Requires level 45 Mining.
 
Fairy Iceheart
- Looted from Winter Court fairies in the Fae Realm, including Winter Court Scout and Winter Court Soldier.

Trivia

Up until the June 9, 2017 Update, first learning Fire Magic was the only path to unlock Ice Magic. The special event that weekend was the first opportunity to obtain Bunny Juice and Un-Bunny Juice.
